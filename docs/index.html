Fear & Greed Index FAQs
What is the WSB Fear & Greed Index?
The WSB Fear & Greed Index measures the collective mood of Reddit's WallStreetBets (WSB) forum, one of the most active retail trading forums for US stock and options markets. When WSB is greedy, retail traders are piling in and chasing momentum. When fearful, they're pulling back, cutting exposure, and bracing for further downside. The index gives you a single number (0–100) to cut through the noise.
How is WSB Fear & Greed Calculated?
Each day's WSB posts are analyzed by AI and classified as bullish, bearish or neutral. The final score blends a quantitative signal (share of bullish vs. bearish posts, weighted 30%) with AI's holistic read of the batch (weighted 70%), covering its overall energy, sarcasm, and tone. WSB communicates heavily in irony and memes, so raw post counts alone can miss the actual sentiment.
How Often is the Index Updated?
The index is recalculated twice daily at 5:00 AM and 8:00 PM HKT. Each update analyzes all WSB posts from the prior 24 hours, typically 50–100 posts.
How to Use the WSB Fear & Greed Index?
Use it as a contrarian signal, not trading advice. Extreme Greed (80+) often means retail is overextended; Extreme Fear (below 20) can signal capitulation. Combined with the trending themes and hot tickers, it gives you a ground-level view of where retail attention is concentrated.
